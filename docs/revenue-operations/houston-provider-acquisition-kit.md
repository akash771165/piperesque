# PIPERESQUE — HOUSTON PROVIDER ACQUISITION KIT

This toolkit contains the operational materials needed to manually prospect, contact, qualify, and onboard independent plumbing contractors in Greater Houston.

---

## 1. PROVIDER QUALIFICATION CHECKLIST

Before sending referrals to any local plumbing business, verify the following credentials:

* [ ] **TSBPE Licensing:** Verify Responsible Master Plumber (RMP) license number on the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners public portal (`https://tsbpe.texas.gov/license-search/`). Confirm license status is "Current / Active" with zero disciplinary suspensions.
* [ ] **General Liability Insurance:** Verify minimum $1,000,000 commercial liability coverage (standard Texas contractor requirement).
* [ ] **Local Houston Dispatch Area:** Confirm which specific territories the provider actively dispatches trucks to:
  - *North Houston:* Spring, The Woodlands, Tomball, Cypress (ZIPs: 77373, 77379, 77380, 77429, 77433)
  - *West Houston:* Katy, Memorial, Energy Corridor (ZIPs: 77449, 77450, 77494, 77079)
  - *Southwest / Fort Bend:* Sugar Land, Missouri City, Richmond (ZIPs: 77478, 77479, 77459)
  - *Southeast:* Pearland, Pasadena, Clear Lake (ZIPs: 77581, 77584, 77502)
  - *Central:* Heights, Montrose, Galleria, Bellaire (ZIPs: 77007, 77008, 77019, 77096)
* [ ] **Response Time Capability:** Provider has a live dispatcher or office manager able to answer calls within 3 rings or return web inquiries within 15 minutes during standard hours.
* [ ] **Service Capabilities:** Confirm whether provider handles:
  - Residential Service & Repair (Water heaters, drain cleaning, leaks)
  - Sewer Camera Inspection & Hydro-Jetting
  - Under-Slab Leak Detection & Rerouting / Tunneling
  - Commercial / Food Service Plumbing

## 2. OWNER-TO-OWNER PHONE OUTREACH SCRIPT

**Target:** Owner or General Manager of mid-sized Houston plumbing companies (3 to 10 trucks).  
**Tone:** Direct, professional, non-salesy, peer-to-peer.

> **Caller (PipeResque Owner):**  
> *"Hi, my name is [Your Name], owner of PipeResque. We run an informational plumbing guide and referral site for homeowners in Greater Houston (www.piperesque.com).  
>  
> We receive inbound calls and inquiries daily from homeowners in [e.g., Katy / Sugar Land / North Houston] who have active water heater issues, drain backups, and slab leaks.  
>  
> Rather than letting these leads route through national call centers that charge high markups, I’m looking to partner directly with one or two reputable, licensed local master plumbers in [Territory] who have truck capacity and want exclusive, high-intent service calls.  
>  
> Is the owner or general manager available for a quick 2-minute conversation about routing local customer inquiries to your dispatch team?"*

### Conversation Talking Points With Owner:
* **The Problem With National Platforms (Angi, Thumbtack):**  
  *"We don't sell the same lead to 5 competing plumbers who then race to call the customer. When someone calls or submits through PipeResque, the referral is 100% exclusive to your company."*
* **The Economics:**  
  *"We charge a flat referral fee of $55 to $65 only on qualified customer inquiries that you actually connect with. If it's outside your service area, a wrong number, or someone looking for free phone advice, you don't pay a dime."*
* **Trial Period:**  
  *"I'm happy to route the first 3 customer inquiries to your dispatch line at zero cost so your team can test the lead quality before we sign anything. If you like the customer caliber, we set up a simple weekly invoice."*
