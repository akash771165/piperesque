### 1. PURPOSE AND RELATIONSHIP OF PARTIES
Company operates an informational web portal that introduces property owners in Greater Houston seeking plumbing repairs, diagnostics, and installations to independent third-party plumbing contractors. Contractor is an independently licensed and insured plumbing contractor wishing to receive customer referral inquiries from Company.

**INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS:** The parties acknowledge that Company is NOT an employer, partner, joint venturer, or plumbing contractor. Company does not supervise, direct, warrant, or control the plumbing services performed by Contractor. Contractor is solely responsible for all diagnostics, estimates, permits, plumbing work, warranties, customer invoicing, and compliance with the Texas Plumbing License Law (Occupations Code Chapter 1301).

---

### 2. DEFINITION OF A QUALIFIED REFERRAL (LEAD)
A referral is deemed a **"Billable Qualified Lead"** only if all of the following criteria are satisfied:
1. **Geographic Coverage:** The property location is within Contractor's agreed service territory (e.g., designated Harris, Fort Bend, or Montgomery County ZIP codes).
2. **Valid Plumbing Intent:** The prospective customer is seeking plumbing services within Contractor's scope of work (e.g., water heaters, drain cleaning, leak detection, sewer repair, or repiping).
3. **Genuine Contact Information:** The prospective customer provides a verifiable name, phone number, and physical property address.
4. **Verifiable Connection:**
   - **For Telephone Referrals:** The inbound call is answered by Contractor or Contractor's dispatch team and the conversation lasts at least **ninety (90) seconds**, or Contractor successfully connects with the customer via outbound callback within four (4) business hours.
   - **For Digital Web Referrals:** The customer submits an online inquiry with explicit written consent to be contacted by Contractor, and Contractor is successfully delivered the lead via secure email or SMS notification.

**NON-BILLABLE INQUIRIES:** Contractor shall NOT be billed for:
- Solicitation, spam, or sales calls.
- Inquiries for services outside Contractor's declared scope (e.g., HVAC or electrical).
- Properties located outside Contractor's agreed service ZIP codes.
- Duplicate inquiries from the same customer within a thirty (30) day window.
- Wrong numbers or disconnected lines where customer cannot be reached after three (3) documented attempts within 24 hours.

### 4. ATTRIBUTION, DISPUTE WINDOW & BILLING RECONCILIATION
1. **Lead Ledger:** Company shall maintain an electronic ledger recording each Lead ID, timestamp, customer phone/email, service category, and delivery receipt.
2. **Weekly / Monthly Invoicing:** Company shall provide an itemized invoice on the 1st and 15th of each calendar month.
3. **Dispute Window:** Contractor has **five (5) business days** from invoice receipt to submit written dispute requests for any non-qualifying lead (providing call logs or explanation of disqualification). Undisputed invoices become final and payable within **ten (10) business days** of invoice date.

---

### 5. CUSTOMER CONSENT & DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
Company warrants that all customer inquiries delivered to Contractor have provided affirmative consent to have their contact details transmitted to an independent plumbing service provider in accordance with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) and Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). Contractor agrees to use customer information exclusively to respond to the requested plumbing inquiry and not to resell or distribute customer data to any third party.

---

### 6. CONTRACTOR REPRESENTATIONS & LICENSING
Contractor covenants and warrants that:
1. Contractor maintains an active, unencumbered Master Plumber license issued by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE).
2. Contractor maintains commercial general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence.
3. Contractor shall provide Company with proof of licensing and Certificate of Insurance (COI) annually.

---

### 7. TERM AND TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon **seven (7) days written notice** via email. All qualified leads delivered prior to the effective date of termination remain payable in accordance with Section 3.
