**Reasoning:** These target non-emergency, non-revenue-generating keywords with <1% search volume. Deleting them frees up crawl budget for high-intent pages.

### Pruning (Smart Focus)
**Cities Reduced from 14 → 10:**
- **KEPT:** Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, Pearland, Pasadena, Missouri City, Richmond, Spring, The Woodlands
- **REMOVED:** Tomball, Conroe, League City (+ others with low search volume)

**Reasoning:** 80/20 principle. Top 10 suburbs drive 80% of plumbing leads. Removing others reduces duplicate content without revenue loss.

### Redirects (Zero Link Juice Lost)
**24 Permanent 301 Redirects in `next.config.ts`:**
- Individual location pages → Combined service+location pages
- Deleted blogs → Most relevant service page or blog
- Removed cities → Service areas page or home

**Impact:** Any old links pointing to deleted pages automatically redirect to relevant content. No 404s. No SEO penalty.
